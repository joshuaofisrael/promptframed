## North star
Build a recognizable night-landscape print brand people follow for the *feeling*, then buy when they want that window on their wall — without inventory, without paid ads until organic proves demand.

**12‑month outcome (directional):**
- Instagram: consistent identity + weekly buying habits from the audience
- Site: compounding organic search for Night Windows pieces + “AI art print / moonlit poster” clusters (SEO growth operator rules)
- Revenue: repeatable poster/frame orders via Printful; ads only after JI Ventures monetize thresholds (trailing 7‑day non‑bot ≥ 10k OR yesterday ≥ 2k)

---

## Positioning (never dilute this)
**One line:** Night Windows — cinematic night landscapes as posters.

**Public voice:** short, romantic, specific. No “AI” / “ChatGPT” in captions (disclosure lives on site About/footer).

**Always CTA:** Want this on your wall? Poster link in bio / buy at the link.

**What we are not:** meme AI spam, random style churn, daily article farm, discount poster mill.

## Channel strategy (priority order)

### 1. Instagram (primary demand engine) — Year 1 spine
**Cadence:** 3–5 feed posts/week from the daily 5‑image production (not all five every day publicly; curate the best 3–5). Stories most days.

**Content pillars (rotate):**
| Pillar | Format | Job |
|---|---|---|
| One Window | Single full piece + mood caption + CTA | Sell the feeling |
| Which window tonight? | Carousel / poll of 2–3 | Engagement → DM/reply with buy link |
| Detail → reveal | Story/Reel crop then full | Thumb-stop + click |
| Room mock | Art on bedroom/office wall | Converts “pretty” → “mine” |
| Series drops | “Night Windows No. 0X” | Collectability |

**Growth plays (organic first):**
- Collab/share with interior, travel, and cozy-aesthetic accounts (gift a free print for a story tag once Printful works — owner approve spend)
- Save-worthy carousels (“10 windows for rainy nights”)
- Consistent visual grid (same crop, same moonlit palette) so the profile *looks* like a brand in 3 seconds
- Link in bio → https://moonlitwindows.com/ (gallery home) — never change CTA language

**Paid (later):** only after organic posts repeatedly drive purchases; test $5–10/day boosts on top converting pieces, US shipping geo if Printful Quick Store is US-only.

### 2. SEO / site (compounding asset) — JI Ventures operator rules
Daily highest-EV action; **no article quota**.

**Phase A (weeks 1–4):** technical + piece pages  
- Fast Pages, sitemap, OG images, unique titles/meta per piece  
- Strong piece URLs people paste into Instagram  
- Guide live: “How to turn ChatGPT art into a framed poster” (trust + long-tail)

**Phase B (months 2–4):** topic cluster  
- Night landscape print intents, room-by-room (“bedroom moonlit poster”), size/frame guides  
- Internal links piece ↔ guide ↔ buy  
- Compound winners only (pages gaining impressions get more investment)

**Phase C (months 4–12):** authority  
- Series landing pages (Night Windows Volume 1, Northern Lights set, Mediterranean set)  
- Comparison/utility pages that beat thin competitors  
- Cloudflare analytics + DailyViews sheet; ads hold until thresholds

### 3. Email / SMS (owned audience) — start when first sales land
- “New window this week” for buyers and Instagram link-in-bio email capture  
- One email/week max; same CTA; no blast culture

### 4. Secondary (optional, after Instagram + SEO work)
- Pinterest (evergreen print discovery — strong for wall art)  
- TikTok/Reels only if detail→reveal and room-mock formats win on IG first  
- Do **not** scatter across 6 platforms early

## Product & offer ladder
1. **Hero:** 12×18 or 20×30 enhanced matte poster (Instagram default ask)  
2. **Upsell:** framed black / oak  
3. **Bundle later:** “Three Windows” set discount (after 30+ SKUs)  
4. **Never** race to cheapest; price as art (see PRINTFUL_SETUP retail bands)

**Quality gate:** upscale/regenerate to print-safe resolution before advertising large framed sizes; sample order once before scaling ads.

# Prompt Framed — Joshua Israel Ventures LLC

## Mission
Online art gallery of ChatGPT / AI-drawn images. Visitors browse the gallery, buy posters or framed prints, and we fulfill via print-on-demand (mail-out for a charge). Market primarily via Instagram + organic SEO.

## Product flow
1. ChatGPT (or other AI) draws art → export/upload into `gallery/`
2. Site shows artwork pages with SEO + buy CTAs
3. Checkout via Printful (or Gelato) for poster / framed print → print + ship to customer
4. Instagram markets new drops linking to product pages

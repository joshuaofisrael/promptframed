How to choose lake wall art for your room

Lake house or cabin. If the room already looks out on water, pick a scene that echoes it rather than competes with it: Lakeside Cabin with its glowing window between the pines, or the misty Scottish Highlands Loch. A framed 12×18 print suits a mantel, an entryway or the wall above a bunk.

Bedroom. The stillest water reads calmest: Lake Louise and Patagonia Lake Peaks, where turquoise water mirrors the mountains, or Japanese Temple Pond with the moon reflected among stone lanterns.

Living room or a feature wall. Lit villages carry well across a room: Bellagio on Lake Como, Hallstatt and Lake Bled’s island church. To make the lake fill the wall, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers measuring and wall prep.

Office or hallway. Waterfalls bring movement to a narrow wall: Iguazu Falls tier after tier, Staubbach Falls above Lauterbrunnen, or Plitvice’s terraced lakes.

A trip to remember. If you rowed out to Bled’s island, took the ferry to Bellagio or walked the Plitvice boardwalks, the place-named prints are made for that. Pairs work well too: Hallstatt with Lake Bled for an Alpine lakes wall, or Iguazu with Plitvice for waterfalls.

Looking for peaks rather than water? The mountain wall art collection has the Matterhorn, the Dolomites and Mount Fuji. Not sure about size? The print size guide compares the poster, framed print and mural.

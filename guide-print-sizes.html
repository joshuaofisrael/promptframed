How to choose a Night Windows size: poster, framed print or wall mural

Every Night Windows piece comes in three formats: a 12×18 in poster ($29), the same 12×18 print in a black frame ($69), or a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel-and-stick wall mural ($149). Shipping is included on all three. Choose 12×18 for a desk, shelf, hallway or a pair of prints, and the mural when you want the scene to fill a wall.

Night Windows pieces are meant to feel like a window into somewhere you wish you were. Size changes that feeling: a 12×18 print is a small window you look into up close, while a 4×6 ft mural turns one scene into the whole view. Use the table and room notes below to pick the format that suits your wall.

12×18 poster or framed print — intimate

Choose this when you sit close: a reading chair, a hallway landing, or a pair flanking a mirror. Detail in wildflowers and lantern windows stays crisp at arm’s length. On a large blank wall a single 12×18 can look small, so hang two or three side by side, or choose the mural instead.

The poster and the framed print are the same 12×18 image. Pick the poster if you already have a frame or want to choose your own; pick the framed print if you want it to arrive in a black frame.

4×6 ft wall mural — the statement

The mural is for when you want the mountain, lake or coastline to fill the wall rather than hang on it: behind a bed, above a long sofa, or as the backdrop to a desk. It is printed on peel-and-stick polyester, so there is no paste, and it removes cleanly from most smooth painted walls. Each mural is printed to order and shipped by post in 1–2 weeks, shipping included.

Measure first: the mural covers 48×72 in, so check that the wall has that much clear, smooth painted surface before you order. The peel and stick wall murals collection shows the scenes that work best at wall size, with measuring and wall-prep tips.

Hanging tips

  • Match the furniture, not the whole wall. Above a sofa or bed, a pair or trio of 12×18 prints reads as one group; keep the group under about two-thirds of the furniture’s width so it feels anchored rather than floating.
  • Leave breathing room. Leave a few inches of wall between a frame and shelves, TVs, or door frames so moonlight edges do not collide with clutter.
  • Step back for the mural. A wall-size print is meant to be seen from across the room. From a few steps back the scene reads as a window; pressed close, fine detail is softer than on a 12×18 print, as with any image printed at wall size.

About the frame

The framed print comes in a black frame. Black keeps the deep blue-green night tones cinematic and works well against light walls, and it matches rooms that already have black metal window frames or dark furniture. If you would rather use a wood or white frame, order the poster and frame it yourself.

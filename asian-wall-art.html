How to choose Asian wall art for your room

Bedroom. Go for stillness: Kyoto Bamboo Moon with its soft green grove, Japanese Temple Pond with the moon in the water, or the lantern-lit junks of Ha Long Bay.

Living room or a feature wall. Big, recognisable scenes carry across a room: Mount Fuji and the Chureito Pagoda, the Great Wall at Mutianyu and the Taj Mahal. To make one fill the wall, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers measuring and wall prep.

Meditation corner, yoga room or study. The temple pieces are the most contemplative: Angkor Wat reflected in its lotus pond, the stupas of Bagan, or mossy steps in Bali Jungle Temple.

Office or hallway. Layered mountain scenes read well from a distance: the Li River near Guilin, Zhangjiajie’s pillars or Moonlit Rice Terraces.

A trip to remember. If you hiked to the Chureito Pagoda, walked the wall at Mutianyu or visited Angkor, the place-named prints are made for that. Pairs work well too: Fuji with Kyoto for a Japan wall, or Guilin with Ha Long Bay for karst country.

Questions about these Asian and Japanese prints

What sizes and formats are available? Every piece comes as a 12×18 in enhanced matte poster ($35), the same 12×18 print in a black frame ($69), or a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural on removable polyester ($149). Shipping is included on all three.

Are these real places? Most are named places reimagined at night: the Chureito Pagoda below Mount Fuji, a Kyoto bamboo grove, the Great Wall at Mutianyu, the Li River near Guilin, Zhangjiajie, the Taj Mahal, Angkor Wat, Bagan and Ha Long Bay. Japanese Temple Pond, Bali Jungle Temple and Moonlit Rice Terraces evoke those landscapes rather than one exact viewpoint.

Is there Japanese wall art for a bedroom? Yes. Kyoto Bamboo Moon and Japanese Temple Pond are the calmest pieces in the collection, with soft greens, still water and mist. Mount Fuji Pagoda Moon has more colour from the cherry blossoms and pagoda, so it suits a living room or hallway as well.

Will the watermark be on my print? No. The previews on this site carry a small Night Shade Art watermark to discourage copying; your print is made from a clean file without it.

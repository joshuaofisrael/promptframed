Moon Poster for Bedroom: Calm Night Windows Wall Art

$35 poster · $69 framed · 12×18 · $149 peel & stick wall mural (4×6 ft) · shipping included

A moon poster for a bedroom should feel like a quiet night window: deep blues and soft moonlight, not bright midday colour. Night Windows prints come as a 12×18 poster ($35), a black-framed 12×18 print ($69), or a 4×6 ft peel-and-stick wall mural ($149), with shipping included. Use a 12×18 above a nightstand; use the mural when you want the scene behind the bed.

Size and viewing distance in a bedroom

From the pillow you sit about 2–5 ft from a nightstand print, so a 12×18 framed piece holds detail without dominating the wall. The wall behind the bed is usually seen from across the room: that is where a 4×6 ft mural reads as a window rather than a postage stamp. The print size guide compares poster, frame and mural in more detail, including hanging height.

Leave a few inches of clear wall around a framed print so it does not collide with lamps, shelves or a headboard. For a mural, measure a clear 48×72 in of smooth painted wall before you order; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers prep and removal.

Which themes suit a bedroom

Still lakes and misty mountains keep the darkest, calmest blues: Lake Louise, Patagonia Lake Peaks, Cradle Mountain above Dove Lake, or a Highlands loch. Browse the lake and waterfall and mountain collections.

Japanese gardens and bamboo add soft greens and mist without busy streets: Kyoto Bamboo Moon and Japanese Temple Pond sit well in a quiet room. See the Asian wall art collection.

Quiet canals and lavender bring gentle European light: Bruges Canal Moon, Hallstatt or Provence Lavender Moon. See the European wall art collection.

Busy cliff villages and neon-bright coasts can feel more awake; save those for a living room or hallway unless you specifically want that energy by the bed.

Questions about moon wall art in a bedroom

What size moon poster works in a bedroom? A 12×18 in poster ($35) or black-framed print ($69) suits a nightstand, small dresser or the wall beside a bed, viewed from about 2–5 ft. For the wall behind the bed, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel-and-stick wall mural ($149). The print size guide compares all three.

Which Night Windows themes feel calmest in a bedroom? Still lakes, misty mountains, soft Japanese gardens and quiet canals. Bright coastal villages and busy old towns can work too, but the softest night blues and greens are the easiest to fall asleep under.

Is a moon wall mural too dark for a bedroom? Moonlit scenes are dark by nature, which is why they suit bedrooms. Keep a warm bedside lamp so the room still feels inviting; the mural reads as a night window rather than a bright focal colour.

Will the watermark be on my print? No. Site previews carry a small Night Shade Art watermark; your print is made from a clean file without it.

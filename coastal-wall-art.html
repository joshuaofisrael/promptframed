How to choose coastal wall art for your room

Beach house or seaside flat. Echo the view without repeating it: the lit terraces of Amalfi Terrace Night or Manarola bring the village, not just the water. A framed 12×18 print suits a hallway, a mantel or the wall beside a window.

Bedroom. Quiet water reads calmest: Reine in Lofoten with its mirror-still fjord, Ha Long Bay with lantern-lit junks, or the tidal shimmer around Mont Saint-Michel.

Living room or a feature wall. Big cliffs carry across a room: the Cliffs of Moher, Bixby Creek Bridge on Big Sur and Santorini’s caldera. To make the coast fill the wall, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers measuring and wall prep.

Office or study. The northern scenes are moodier and less busy: Northern Lights Fjord, Norwegian Stave Church Fjord or Iceland Black Sand Moon.

A trip to remember. If you drove Highway 1, walked the Cinque Terre trail or stood on the Cliffs of Moher, the place-named prints are made for that. Pairs work well too: Amalfi with Santorini for a Mediterranean wall, or Lofoten with the Faroes for the North Atlantic.

Prefer still lakes or high peaks? See the lake and waterfall wall art collection and the mountain wall art collection. Not sure about size? The print size guide compares the poster, framed print and mural.

Questions about these coastal prints

What sizes and formats are available? Every piece comes as a 12×18 in enhanced matte poster ($35), the same 12×18 print in a black frame ($69), or a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural on removable polyester ($149). Shipping is included on all three.

Are these real places? Most are named coasts reimagined at night: the Amalfi Coast, Santorini, Manarola in the Cinque Terre, Dubrovnik on the Adriatic, the Cliffs of Moher, Bixby Creek Bridge on Big Sur, the Na Pali Coast on Kauai, the lagoon of Bora Bora, Mont Saint-Michel, Gásadalur in the Faroe Islands, Reine in Lofoten, Eilean Donan Castle on Loch Duich and Ha Long Bay. Iceland Black Sand Moon and Norwegian Stave Church Fjord evoke those coastlines rather than one exact viewpoint, and Coastal Cliffside Town, Mediterranean Lantern Walk and Northern Lights Fjord are imagined scenes.

Is this beach decor? These are night scenes of seaside towns, ocean cliffs and fjords rather than sunny beach prints; Bora Bora Lagoon Moon comes closest to tropical decor, with palms and overwater bungalows on a moonlit lagoon, and Iceland Black Sand Moon shows a black-sand shore. If you want coastal decor that still feels calm in a bedroom, the moonlight keeps the palette soft and deep blue.

Will the watermark be on my print? No. The previews on this site carry a small Night Shade Art watermark to discourage copying; your print is made from a clean file without it.

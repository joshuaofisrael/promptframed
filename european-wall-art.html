How to choose European wall art for your room

Bedroom. Go for quiet water and soft light: Bruges Canal Moon with its mirrored gables, Hallstatt on its still lake, or the lavender rows of Provence Lavender Moon. More bedroom picks and size tips are in the moon poster for bedroom guide.

Child’s room or nursery. Castles do the storytelling: Neuschwanstein above its misty valley, Eilean Donan on its loch, or Mont Saint-Michel rising from the tide.

Living room or a feature wall. Wide, recognisable views carry across a room: Prague’s Charles Bridge, Dubrovnik’s walled old town or the monasteries of Meteora. To make one fill the wall, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers measuring and wall prep.

Kitchen, dining room or hallway. The countryside and village pieces feel homely: Tuscany Cypress Hills, the cottages of Bibury’s Arlington Row or the lamplit lane in Rothenburg Old Town Moon.

A trip to remember. If you crossed the Charles Bridge, walked Dubrovnik’s walls or drove through Tuscany, the place-named prints are made for that. Pairs work well too: Venice with Bruges or Colmar for a canal wall, Neuschwanstein with Rothenburg for Bavaria, or Sintra’s Pena Palace with Mont Saint-Michel for a castles pair.

Questions about these European castle and old town prints

What sizes and formats are available? Every piece comes as a 12×18 in enhanced matte poster ($35), the same 12×18 print in a black frame ($69), or a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural on removable polyester ($149). Shipping is included on all three.

Are these real places? Most are named places reimagined at night: Neuschwanstein, Hohenzollern Castle in the Swabian Alb, Eilean Donan, the Pena Palace in Sintra, Mont Saint-Michel, Meteora, Prague’s Charles Bridge, Florence’s Ponte Vecchio, the Rozenhoedkaai in Bruges, Colmar’s Petite Venise, Dubrovnik, Rothenburg, Hallstatt, Arlington Row in Bibury and the Glenfinnan Viaduct in the Scottish Highlands. Venetian Canal Moonrise, Provence Lavender Moon and Tuscany Cypress Hills evoke those places rather than one exact viewpoint.

Do you have castle wall art for a child’s room? Yes. Neuschwanstein Castle Moon is the most fairy-tale piece in the series, and Eilean Donan and Mont Saint-Michel have the same storybook feel with calmer, darker water. All of them are soft night scenes with a full moon rather than anything spooky.

Which pieces work as a travel souvenir? The place-named prints make a good reminder of a trip: Prague Charles Bridge Moon, Bruges Canal Moon, Dubrovnik Old Town Moon or Hallstatt Lake Moon for a city break, or Provence and Tuscany for a summer in the countryside.

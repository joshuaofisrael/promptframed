Western & Central Europe Wall Art: Moonlit Old Towns, Canals and Castles

7 moonlit art prints · $35 poster · $69 framed · 12×18 · $149 peel & stick wall mural (4×6 ft) · shipping included

Fairytale Europe after dark: the canals of Bruges and Colmar, Rothenburg’s cobbled streets, Prague’s Charles Bridge, Hohenzollern Castle on its hill, Mont Saint-Michel on the tide and the lavender rows of Provence. Each comes as a 12×18 in poster ($35), the same print in a black frame ($69) or a 4×6 ft peel and stick wall mural ($149), with shipping included.

Warm window light against blue night stone is the theme here, which makes these some of the cosiest pieces in the series. They suit dining rooms, hallways and travel walls, and work well as a set from one trip.

Wall art for renters

You can change a rental wall without losing the deposit. Start with a 12×18 poster or framed print on removable hardware, or cover a feature wall with a peel-and-stick mural.

Why renters need a different plan

Many leases limit holes, nails and paint. That does not mean bare walls. It means choosing art and hardware you can remove, and documenting the wall before and after. Night Windows pieces are sold as a $35 poster, a $69 black-framed 12×18 print, or a $149 peel-and-stick 4×6 ft mural, with shipping included — formats that map cleanly onto renter constraints.

Option 1: Poster or framed print, no permanent holes

Use manufacturer-rated removable adhesive strips or hooks sized for the frame weight. Keep the print modest (12×18 is easy to level and move). Hang with the same height habits in our hanging height guide. When you leave, warm the adhesive gently and pull slowly so paint stays put.

Option 2: Peel-and-stick mural as a feature wall

Our peel-and-stick murals turn one wall into a night window without rails or nails. They suit bedrooms and living rooms where a landlord forbids drilling but allows temporary finishes. Clean, dry, smooth walls work best; textured or freshly painted walls need extra care. Plan removal day: peel slowly from a corner and avoid yanking across paint edges.

What to pick for small apartments

  • Studio / one-bed: one calm lake or mountain print above the sofa, or a mural behind the bed.
  • Shared walls: softer night blues from lake wall art read quieter than bright coastal scenes.
  • Moving often: posters travel flat; framed prints need a box; murals roll.

Deposit-safe habits

Photograph walls on move-in and move-out. Stick to removable hardware within its weight rating. Avoid brick or crumbly plaster with adhesives that need a smooth surface. If your lease bans wall coverings entirely, keep art on freestanding ledges instead of the wall.

FAQ

Can renters hang Night Windows art? Yes. Use removable strips for 12×18 posters or framed prints, or choose a peel-and-stick mural that installs without nails.

Will a peel-and-stick mural damage paint? On sound, properly cured paint it is designed to remove cleanly when peeled slowly. Always test a corner and follow your lease.

What is the cheapest renter-friendly option? The $35 12×18 poster with removable hanging hardware. Shipping is included.

What if my lease bans wall coverings? Skip murals and keep art on shelves or freestanding ledges, or confirm written permission before installing anything adhesive.

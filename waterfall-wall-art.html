How to choose waterfall wall art for your room

Hallway, stair wall or a narrow space. A tall waterfall fills a slim wall the way a window would: Skógafoss with its staircase path climbing beside the spray, Staubbach Falls above Lauterbrunnen, or Múlafossur at Gásadalur dropping straight toward the sea.

Bedroom. Cool greens and soft white water read calmest at night: Kirkjufellsfoss below Kirkjufell or the gentle terraced falls of Plitvice. More bedroom picks and size tips are in the moon poster for bedroom guide.

Living room, office or a feature wall. The big falls have the most energy: Iguazu tier after tier, Victoria Falls with a moonbow in its spray, and Havasu Falls, where red canyon rock frames a turquoise pool. To let a waterfall fill the wall, the same artwork comes as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural; the peel and stick wall murals collection covers measuring and wall prep.

Bathroom or spa corner. Water suits water. A framed print of Moonlit Waterfall or Plitvice brings the sound of a gorge to mind without adding colour that fights the tiles; hang it away from direct splashes.

A trip to remember. If you drove Iceland’s Ring Road, hiked down to Havasupai or stood in the spray at Victoria Falls, the place-named prints are made for that. Pairs work well too: Skógafoss with Kirkjufellsfoss for Iceland, or Iguazu with Victoria Falls for the world’s great falls.

Prefer still water? The lake and waterfall wall art collection has Lake Como, Lake Bled, Hallstatt and Moraine Lake, and the mountain wall art collection has the Matterhorn, the Dolomites and Mount Fuji. For sea cliffs and harbours, see the coastal wall art collection. Not sure about size? The print size guide compares the poster, framed print and mural.

Questions about these waterfall prints

What sizes and formats are available? Every piece comes as a 12×18 in enhanced matte poster ($35), the same 12×18 print in a black frame ($69), or a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel and stick wall mural on removable polyester ($149). Shipping is included on all three.

Are these real waterfalls? Eight are named falls reimagined at night: Skógafoss and Kirkjufellsfoss in Iceland, Múlafossur at Gásadalur in the Faroe Islands, Staubbach Falls above Lauterbrunnen in Switzerland, the terraced falls of Plitvice in Croatia, Iguazu on the Argentina–Brazil border, Havasu Falls in the Grand Canyon in Arizona, and Victoria Falls on the Zambezi between Zambia and Zimbabwe. Moonlit Waterfall is an imagined gorge rather than one exact place.

Can you see a waterfall by moonlight? Yes. Near a full moon, white water and spray catch a surprising amount of light, and at big falls such as Victoria Falls the spray can even form a lunar rainbow, or moonbow. Victoria Falls Moon shows one. The prints keep that soft, silvery night light rather than a bright daytime look.

Which waterfall print suits a bedroom? The calmest are the Icelandic and Faroese falls, Skógafoss, Kirkjufellsfoss and Múlafossur, with cool greens and soft white water, and Plitvice, where the falls are small and gentle. Iguazu, Havasu and Victoria Falls have more power and colour, so they suit a living room, an office or a hallway.

Will the watermark be on my print? No. The previews on this site carry a small Night Shade Art watermark to discourage copying; your print is made from a clean file without it.

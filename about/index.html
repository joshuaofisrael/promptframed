The studio

About Prompt Framed

Night Windows is the gallery. Joshua Israel Ventures LLC is the studio behind it. We hang quiet night pictures and offer them as posters you can live with. A framed print is the second way to order the same picture.

Nothing is printed until someone buys it. Then the print shop makes it and mails it. There is no stack of paper waiting in a back room.

What is hanging

The first picture is Moonlit Alpine Meadow: flowers in the foreground, warm windows in the valley, snow farther off. More nights will be added the same way, one page at a time.

A note on the pictures

Art in this gallery may be AI-generated, including pictures made with ChatGPT and similar tools. The studio chooses what hangs, names it, and sells the print. That disclosure also sits in the footer of every page.

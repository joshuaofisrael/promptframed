The path

From a night picture to a poster on the wall.

Five steps. The piece page is the link people tap. The print shop is where the order is paid, printed, and mailed.

  1. The picture joins the gallery. Each Night Windows picture has its own page, large enough to judge on a phone. Moonlit Alpine Meadow is Photo 1. Moonlit Mediterranean Village is Photo 2.
  2. Instagram points here. The caption ends with one sentence: “Want a poster? Buy at the link.” That link opens the piece page.
  3. Buy Poster. The gold button opens secure Stripe checkout for a 12×18 poster ($29, shipping included). This site never sees your card number. Each poster is printed to order and mailed to you.
  4. Framed, when you want the second option. The same picture can be ordered as a framed 12×18 print ($69, shipping included). That is its own checkout button on the piece page.
  5. Wall mural, when you want the whole wall. Every piece is also available as a 4×6 ft (48×72 in) peel-and-stick wall mural ($149, shipping included), with its own checkout button on the piece page. The mural is printed to order on removable polyester and shipped by post, usually within 1–2 weeks. No paste is needed, and it removes cleanly from most smooth painted walls.

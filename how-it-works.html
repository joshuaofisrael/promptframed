The path

From a night picture to a poster on the wall.

Four steps. The piece page is the link people tap. The print shop is where the order is paid, printed, and mailed.

  1. The picture joins the gallery. Each Night Windows picture has its own page, large enough to judge on a phone. Moonlit Alpine Meadow is Photo 1. Moonlit Mediterranean Village is Photo 2.
  2. Instagram points here. The caption ends with one sentence: “Want a poster? Buy at the link.” That link opens the piece page.
  3. Buy Poster. The gold button is the order. When the print shop link is saved, the button opens that product. This site does not take a card number. The shop prints the poster and ships it.
  4. Framed, when you want the second option. The same picture can be ordered as a framed print. That is a separate product link.

Iceland & Nordic Wall Art: Moonlit Fjords, Falls and Black Sand

7 moonlit art prints · $35 poster · $69 framed · 12×18 · $149 peel & stick wall mural (4×6 ft) · shipping included

Every Nordic piece in the Night Windows series in one place: Icelandic waterfalls and black sand, the Faroe Islands, Lofoten and the Norwegian fjords, each under a full moon with thin wispy cloud. Each comes as a 12×18 in poster ($35), the same print in a black frame ($69) or a 4×6 ft peel and stick wall mural ($149), with shipping included.

Northern light is long and low, so these scenes keep cool greens, slate blues and silver water with only a few warm cabin windows. They suit calm bedrooms, Scandi and minimalist rooms, and anyone who has driven Iceland’s Ring Road or the Lofoten islands.

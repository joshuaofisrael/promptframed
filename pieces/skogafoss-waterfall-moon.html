About this Skógafoss poster

Skógafoss is one of Iceland’s best known waterfalls, on the Skógá river near the village of Skógar on the south coast, just off the Ring Road. The river drops about 60 metres over a former sea cliff in a curtain roughly 25 metres wide, and the spray often throws rainbows on sunny days. A staircase of several hundred steps climbs the slope beside the falls to a viewing platform at the top, where the Fimmvörðuháls hiking trail begins.

In the painting

This Night Windows piece shows the broad white curtain pouring over a sheer, moss-covered cliff into rising mist, the staircase path with its railing climbing the slope, the Skógá river running over dark rocks, mossy boulders and white wildflowers in the foreground, and a full moon among thin clouds. It is a painted, moonlit reimagining rather than a photograph of one exact viewpoint.

Where it hangs best

The artwork is a tall 2:3 portrait. The 12×18 poster or black-framed print suits a bedroom, a narrow wall or a hallway, and pairs naturally with Seljalandsfoss, its neighbour along the south coast, for an Iceland wall (see the Iceland and Nordic pieces).

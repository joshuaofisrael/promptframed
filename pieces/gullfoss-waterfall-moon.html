About this Gullfoss poster

Gullfoss, “golden falls”, is on the Hvítá river in southwest Iceland and is one of the stops on the Golden Circle route with Þingvellir and Geysir. The river turns and drops in two stages, of about 11 and 21 metres, into a narrow canyon that runs away from the falls, so from the viewpoints the water seems to vanish into the earth. Plans to dam the river for power in the early 20th century never went ahead, and the falls have been a protected nature reserve since 1979.

In the painting

This Night Windows piece shows the wide tiered falls plunging into a deep, rugged canyon with spray rising, a small turf-roofed stone farmhouse with warm amber windows on the grassy rim, mossy rocks and white wildflowers in the foreground, and a full moon behind thin clouds. It is a painted, moonlit reimagining rather than a photograph of one exact viewpoint.

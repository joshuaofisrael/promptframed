How high to hang wall art

Museum galleries often centre art near 57–60 inches from the floor. At home, adjust for sofa backs, headboards and murals that cover a whole wall.

The 57–60 inch centre rule

Many museums and galleries hang so the vertical centre of a work sits about 57 to 60 inches (145–152 cm) above the finished floor. That puts the middle of the image near average adult eye level when you are standing. For a 12×18 inch Night Windows poster or black-framed print, that usually means the top of the frame sits a little above eye level and the bottom hangs clear of switches and chair backs.

Use a tape measure from the floor to the planned centre of the print, not to the nail. Mark lightly, then check from across the room before you commit.

Above a sofa or console

When art hangs above furniture, leave roughly 6 to 12 inches between the top of the sofa back (or console) and the bottom of the frame. A gap that is too large makes the print float; a gap that is too tight feels cramped. On a long sofa, one 12×18 print often looks best slightly off-centre toward a lamp or paired with a second piece rather than stranded in the exact middle of a huge wall.

Above a bed

Keep the bottom of a framed print a comfortable distance above the headboard, typically in that same 6–12 inch band, so pillows do not brush the frame. For the wall behind a bed, many people prefer our 4×6 ft peel-and-stick mural instead of a small frame: the mural is the backdrop, so hanging height is about aligning the scene with the headboard rather than finding a nail at eye level. Murals versus small prints

A 12×18 print is a point of focus. A 4×6 ft mural is architecture. Hang the print with the centre rule; install the mural so the horizon or main motif sits where you look when seated or lying in bed. Quick checklist

  • Stand back 6–8 ft and check the centre before drilling.
  • Above furniture: aim for 6–12 inches of air under the frame.
  • Use two hooks on wider frames so the print stays level.
  • Renters: removable strips rated for the frame weight; peel-and-stick murals avoid holes entirely when walls are sound.

FAQ

How high should I hang a 12×18 poster? Centre it near 57–60 inches from the floor when you view it standing, or leave 6–12 inches above a sofa back or headboard when it hangs over furniture.

Do murals use the same hanging height? Not really. A 4×6 ft mural covers most of a wall, so align the scene with how you sit or sleep rather than a single nail at eye level.

How much space above a sofa? About 6 to 12 inches between the sofa back and the bottom of the frame keeps the print connected to the furniture without crowding it.

Will Night Windows frames arrive ready to hang? Framed prints ship ready to hang; posters can use frames you already own or clips. Murals are peel-and-stick.
